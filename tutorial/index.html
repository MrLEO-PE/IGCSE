Part 1

Get started

Three quick set-up steps: an account for the class leaderboard, a profile picture, and a free key that switches on AI marking.

1

Create your account

Tap 👤 Sign in (top right), then Create one. Enter:
  • your name (this is what your class will see on the leaderboard)
  • the class code your teacher gives you. Type it exactly as given, because it decides which class leaderboard you join
  • your email and a password (at least 6 characters)
Next time, just sign in with the same email and password.
2

Add a profile picture

Once you're signed in, your first name shows in the top-right corner. On the Account page, tap the 📷 button on your avatar to choose a file or take a photo. The photo must clearly show your face. It appears next to your name on the leaderboard.
3

Turn on AI marking (free key)

Tap 🔑 API key, then 📖 How do I get a key?
  • Open console.groq.com/keys and sign up (free, no credit card needed)
  • Click Create API Key and copy the code that starts with gsk_
  • Paste it in the box and tap Save
The key is stored only in your browser. Without a key you can still read the model answers, but your answers won't be marked.
Part 2

Learn and practise

Work through the chapters with instant feedback, then use spaced review, the syllabus checklist, diagrams and flashcards to fill the gaps.

4

Pick a chapter

The home screen lists every chapter of the Collins Cambridge book, with the number of questions and diagrams in each. The bar at the top shows your overall progress, and each chapter card shows how many of its questions you've answered. You can switch chapter at any time from the 📚 Chapters tab.
5

Answer questions and get marked

In 📋 Questions, write your answer in the box. Stuck? Open the 💡 Examiner tip. Tap Check answer ↗ and the AI examiner gives you a score and feedback: green means correct, amber partly correct, red needs more work. Your answers are saved automatically, and the counters at the top track how many questions you've answered and your average score.
6

Compare with the model answer

Tap Model answer to see the full mark-scheme answer. If you've already written something, it stays blurred until you click it, so try the question yourself before you look. Use 🖨 Print worksheet for a paper copy of the chapter, or 🗑 Clear saved to start the chapter again. Questions marked ⭐ Extension go beyond the core syllabus. They are there for extra practice and never come up in the quiz.
7

Review your weak spots

Any question you score under 80% on comes back later for review (spaced repetition). When some are due, an amber banner appears. Tap Review to practise just those questions. Score well and each question comes back less often.
8

Track the whole syllabus

The ✅ Syllabus tab lists every point in the Cambridge IGCSE PE syllabus, coloured by your latest marked answers: green = secure, amber = getting there, red = needs work, grey = not started. Tap Practise next to any point to answer just its questions, or 🎯 Practise my weakest points to revise what needs it most. Sign in so your teacher can see your progress too.
9

Label the diagrams

In 🧩 Diagrams, tap a label (it turns green), then tap the numbered spot where it belongs. On a computer you can also drag and drop labels. Hover over a label (or tap it on a phone) to see its definition in the blue box. Tap Check labels ↗ to see which ones you got right, or Reset to try again.
10

Revise key terms with flashcards

In 🃏 Flashcards, tap a card to flip it and see the definition. Filter the cards by chapter, 🔀 Shuffle them to test yourself, and use ↺ Reset all to turn them all face down again. The counter shows how many cards you've revealed.
Part 3

Test yourself

Sit a 10-question practice exam, submit it, and see how you rank in your class.

11

Take a practice exam

Open 🎲 Quiz, choose a chapter (or All chapters) and tap Start practice exam. You get 10 random questions from the core syllabus. Write your answer and tap Check answer ↗. Once it's marked, the answer is locked, so you can't change it after seeing the model answer. Then tap Next question →. Your running total is shown at the top right.
12

Submit and see your results

After the 10th question (or when you tap End quiz), you see your total marks and percentage, with a score for every question. If you're signed in, your result is sent to the leaderboard automatically. Only your best score counts. Tap Try again to get a new set of questions.
13

Climb the leaderboard

🏆 Leaderboard ranks everyone with your class code by their best quiz percentage. Use the menu to switch between All chapters and a single chapter.
For teachers

See the whole class at a glance

👩‍🏫

The class dashboard

Teacher accounts get a 👩‍🏫 Class tab. Pick a class to see:
  • how many students were active this week and the class average
  • the syllabus points to reteach, weakest first
  • the most-missed questions
  • every student's average, last activity and weakest point, and their own syllabus checklist in one tap
Only accounts the school registers as teachers can open it; students only ever see their own results.
